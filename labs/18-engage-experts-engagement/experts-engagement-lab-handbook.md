# Experts Connect Lab — General Handbook

**Lab:** Experts Engagement Lab (ECL)  
**Track:** 17 — Ask Me Anything  
**Learning Intent:** **ENGAGE**  
**Maturity Stage:** Beginner  
**Core Intention:** Enable interaction with experts and real-world practitioners.  
**Student Outcome:** Learn through engagement and experience sharing.

> **No one becomes great alone.** ECL develops the ability to learn directly from practitioners, ask meaningful questions, listen deeply, capture experience and convert conversations into action.

---

# 1. Purpose

ECL is the **human experience and practitioner-learning layer** of the 20-Lab ecosystem.

It helps learners move from:

**Question → Conversation → Experience → Insight → Application → Relationship**

The objective is not networking for its own sake. It is to **shorten the learning curve by learning from people who have already walked the path**.

The ECL loop is:

**PREPARE → ASK → LISTEN → PROBE → CAPTURE → REFLECT → APPLY → FOLLOW UP → CONNECT → SHARE**

---

# 2. ECL Philosophy

1. **Engage is your intent** — learn through interaction with experts and practitioners.
2. **Come with real questions** — preparation increases learning quality.
3. **Listen more than you speak** — experience is the primary learning asset.
4. **Honor experience humbly** — approach practitioners with respect and openness.
5. **Capture and apply** — turn conversation into action.
6. **Begin where you are** — beginners should ask without hesitation.
7. **Connect across the 11 GICS sectors** — broaden perspective through diverse practitioners.
8. **Connection serves the mission** — shared experience multiplies collective capability.

| Follow-up | What should happen afterward? |

# 6. Question Architecture

Strong questions progress from context to insight.

### Level 1 — Experience
“What have you seen in real projects?”

### Level 2 — Decision
“How did you decide between the alternatives?”

### Level 3 — Trade-off
“What did you sacrifice to achieve that outcome?”

### Level 4 — Failure
“What went wrong, and what did you learn?”

### Level 5 — Pattern
“What do you see repeatedly across organizations?”

### Level 6 — Advice
“What would you do differently if starting today?”

### Level 7 — Future
“What is changing that professionals should prepare for?”

Avoid questions that can be answered by a basic search when the purpose of the session is to access **experience and judgment**.

---

# 7. AMA Framework

Use:

**Context → Question → Listen → Clarify → Probe → Capture → Apply**

For a live AMA:

1. Research the expert.
2. Define your learning objective.
3. Prepare 3–5 high-value questions.
4. Ask concise questions.
5. Listen without interrupting.
6. Probe for examples.
7. Capture lessons.
8. Identify one action.
9. Follow up thoughtfully.

---

# 8. Listening Framework

Practice:

### Active Listening
Focus on what is being said.

### Context Listening
Understand why the expert reached that conclusion.

### Pattern Listening
Notice repeated themes across answers.

### Assumption Listening
Identify beliefs that influence decisions.

### Failure Listening
Pay attention to what did not work.

### Future Listening
Identify emerging changes and signals.

> **The goal is not to collect answers. It is to understand how experienced people think.**

# 10. Experience-to-Action Loop

Use:

**Hear → Understand → Challenge → Adapt → Apply → Observe → Reflect**

- Following up

# 12. Expert Selection

When seeking an expert, consider:

- Relevant experience
- Domain exposure
- Type of problems solved
- Industry context
- Architecture / technology depth
- Leadership experience
- Recent experience
- Ability to explain
- Diversity of perspective

Seek different perspectives rather than relying exclusively on one expert or one organization.

---

# 13. ECL Troubleshooting

### I don't know what to ask
Start with a real problem from your current learning or work.

### The expert gives a generic answer
Ask for a concrete example, trade-off or failure story.

### I am nervous
Prepare your first question and remember that beginner questions are valid.

### The conversation becomes one-sided
Use concise follow-up questions and listen carefully.

### Advice conflicts with another expert
Capture both views and investigate the context behind each.

### The expert is very senior
Respect their time; arrive prepared and make questions specific.

### I learned a lot but did nothing
Convert one insight into an immediate action.

### I cannot maintain the relationship
Follow up occasionally with genuine value rather than forced messages.

### A conversation contains confidential information
Do not record, publish or redistribute restricted information without permission.

ECL requires:

- Respect for expert time
- Confidentiality
- Appropriate attribution
- No unauthorized recording
- No redistribution of private information
- No exploitation of relationships
- No misrepresentation of expert views

- Appropriate consent before quoting or publishing

Human connection creates trust. Protect it.
