## STAR + Evidence

**Situation → Task → Action → Result → Learning → Evidence**

Build genuine stories for complex requisition design, candidate-profile issues, application problems, screening, offers, posting, notifications, RBP, integration, migration, production incidents, cross-module dependencies, stakeholder conflict, global rollout, automation and transformation.

## Scenario Defense Framework

1. Clarify the business outcome.
2. Identify the Recruiting object and lifecycle point.
3. Check standard capability.
4. Check data/configuration dependencies.
5. Check permissions and privacy.
6. Check integration/downstream impact.
7. Define test cases and evidence.
8. Explain assumptions and trade-offs.
