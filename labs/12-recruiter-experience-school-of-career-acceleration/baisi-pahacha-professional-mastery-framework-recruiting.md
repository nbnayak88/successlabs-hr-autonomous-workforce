## Six Themes

**KNOW → DESIGN → DELIVER → SOLVE → INFLUENCE → TRANSFORM**

## Interview Loop

**Understand business outcome → identify Recruiting object/lifecycle → assess standard capability → design → configure → secure → integrate → test → explain trade-offs → improve.**

> Prepare to reason from the business outcome, not recite configuration steps.
