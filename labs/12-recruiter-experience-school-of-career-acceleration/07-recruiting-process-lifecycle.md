# Scenario Category — Recruiting Process Lifecycle

## Scenario Practice

### 1. Approved headcount to offer walkthrough

**Interview prompt:** Explain the Situation, Task, Action and Result. State what you personally owned, the design decisions you made, alternatives considered, validation performed, and what you learned.

**Follow-up probes:**
- What did you personally own?
- Why did you choose that approach?
- What standard capability did you assess first?
- What could fail and how would you detect it?
- What evidence would you show the client?

### 2. Custom screening sequence

**Interview prompt:** Walk through the scenario as a consultant: clarify the business outcome, identify the affected Recruiting objects, assess standard capabilities, define configuration, and explain downstream impact.

**Follow-up probes:**
- What did you personally own?
- Why did you choose that approach?
- What standard capability did you assess first?
- What could fail and how would you detect it?
- What evidence would you show the client?

### 3. Wrong status transition access

**Interview prompt:** Troubleshoot the scenario systematically. Separate business-process, configuration, data, permissions, workflow and integration causes before changing configuration.

**Follow-up probes:**
- What did you personally own?
- Why did you choose that approach?
- What standard capability did you assess first?
- What could fail and how would you detect it?
- What evidence would you show the client?

### 4. Reliable time-to-hire measurement

**Interview prompt:** Describe how you would test and demonstrate production readiness, including positive, negative, boundary, persona, integration and regression scenarios.

**Follow-up probes:**
- What did you personally own?
- Why did you choose that approach?
- What standard capability did you assess first?
- What could fail and how would you detect it?
- What evidence would you show the client?

## Rapid-Fire SME Probes

1. What is the first diagnostic question?
2. What standard capability would you assess before customization?
3. What data dependency could be missed?
4. What negative or boundary test would you add?
5. What proves operational readiness?

## Master Mental Model

**Understand → Decompose → Design/Configure → Secure → Integrate → Test → Release → Measure → Improve**

## Success Criteria

A strong answer connects business outcome, Recruiting configuration, data, security, integrations, testing, stakeholder decisions and operational support.
