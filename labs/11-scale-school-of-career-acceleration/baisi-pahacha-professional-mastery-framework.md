# BAISI PAHACHA™ — The 22-Step Professional Mastery Framework

## Interview Mastery Edition

> **22 Steps. 6 Themes. One Journey from Knowledge to Mastery.**

## 1. Purpose

BAISI PAHACHA™ is the generic SuccessLabs methodology for structured professional and interview mastery. Inspired by the Baisi Pahacha (ବାଇଶି ପାହାଚ) — the traditional 22 steps associated with the Jagannath Temple in Puri — it uses 22 progressive steps as a metaphor for a professional journey from foundational knowledge to strategic mastery.

The framework is technology-, module-, industry-, and role-agnostic. It can be instantiated for SAP SuccessFactors, SAP, non-SAP platforms, Enterprise Architecture, Cloud, Data, AI, Cybersecurity, Integration, Project Management, Product Management, Consulting, Leadership, and other professional domains.

> **The 22-step skeleton remains stable. The domain-specific knowledge, scenarios, terminology, tools and expected depth change.**

## 2. BAISI PAHACHA Brand Meaning

### BAISI

- **B — Business Context:** Understand the business problem, operating model, stakeholders and outcomes.
- **A — Architecture & Application:** Understand technology, product, solution and architectural context.
- **I — Implementation & Integration:** Demonstrate practical delivery and execution capability.
- **S — Solution & Scenario Thinking:** Demonstrate structured reasoning and real-world problem solving.
- **I — Innovation & Intelligence:** Apply automation, AI, emerging technology and continuous improvement appropriately.

### PAHACHA

- **P — Professional Foundations:** Establish domain, technology and role fundamentals.
- **A — Analysis & Assessment:** Analyze requirements, situations, dependencies, risks and alternatives.
- **H — Hands-on Delivery:** Demonstrate implementation and execution.
- **A — Advisory & Influence:** Communicate, collaborate, challenge constructively and influence.
- **C — Critical Problem Solving:** Handle defects, incidents, ambiguity and trade-offs.
- **H — Human & Business Impact:** Connect technology to people, experience and measurable outcomes.
- **A — Advancement & Achievement:** Demonstrate leadership, transformation and future vision.

The acronym is a brand interpretation; Baisi Pahacha remains rooted in the cultural meaning of 22 steps.

## 3. The Six Themes

| Theme | Pahacha | Core Question |
|---|---:|---|
| **KNOW** | 1–4 | What do I know? |
| **DESIGN** | 5–8 | Can I think and design? |
| **DELIVER** | 9–12 | Can I execute? |
| **SOLVE** | 13–16 | Can I solve real problems? |
| **INFLUENCE** | 17–19 | Can I work through people and organizations? |
| **TRANSFORM** | 20–22 | Can I create future value? |

**KNOW → DESIGN → DELIVER → SOLVE → INFLUENCE → TRANSFORM**

## 4. The 22 Pahacha

### THEME 1 — KNOW

**01 — Domain Foundation** — Core concepts, terminology, principles and boundaries.

**02 — Product / Technology Knowledge** — Capabilities, components, features, limitations and relevant releases.

**03 — Business Process & Operating Context** — Users, processes, stakeholders, dependencies and business outcomes.

**04 — Data & Information Model** — Entities, relationships, lifecycle, ownership, information flow and data quality.

### THEME 2 — DESIGN

**05 — Requirement Analysis** — Translate business needs into clear, testable requirements.

**06 — Solution Design** — Select an appropriate solution, balancing standardization, extension, risk and scalability.

**07 — Configuration / Development** — Translate design into a maintainable working solution.

**08 — Architecture & Integration** — Position the solution within the wider application, data, technology, security and integration landscape.

### THEME 3 — DELIVER

**09 — Implementation** — Plan and execute end-to-end delivery.

**10 — Migration & Data Readiness** — Extract, cleanse, transform, load, validate and reconcile data.

**11 — Testing & Quality** — Unit, integration, UAT, regression, negative testing, defects and evidence.

**12 — Release, Go-Live & Support** — Readiness, cutover, production, hypercare and operational transition.

### THEME 4 — SOLVE

**13 — Troubleshooting** — Symptom → Evidence → Isolation → Root Cause → Recovery → Prevention.

**14 — Incident & Defect Management** — Severity, impact, containment, communication, RCA, corrective and preventive action.

**15 — Complex Scenario Thinking** — Reason across business, process, data, technology, integration, security and delivery constraints.

**16 — Optimization & Continuous Improvement** — Improve process, experience, performance, quality, cost, automation and maintainability.

### THEME 5 — INFLUENCE

**17 — Stakeholder Management** — Align global/local, business/IT, functional/technical and competing priorities.

**18 — Communication & Collaboration** — Explain complexity clearly, collaborate across teams and transfer knowledge.

**19 — Advisory & Trusted SME Leadership** — Make recommendations, explain trade-offs, mentor, govern and influence without authority.

### THEME 6 — TRANSFORM

**20 — Automation, AI & Intelligent Delivery** — Apply AI and automation to requirements, documentation, testing, data quality, defects, knowledge and productivity with human accountability.

**21 — Transformation & Business Value** — Connect technology to process improvement, experience, adoption and measurable business outcomes.

**22 — Strategic Mastery & Future Vision** — Articulate future state, roadmap, maturity, emerging technology, operating model and strategic priorities.

## 5. Standard Pahacha Learning Unit

Every domain-specific implementation should contain:

1. **10 scenario-based questions**
2. **STAR answers** — Situation, Task, Action, Result
3. **SME follow-up probes** — Why, alternatives, risks, failures, scale, measurement and lessons
4. **Rapid-fire questions**
5. **A mastery framework**
6. **Anti-patterns / weak-answer behaviors**
7. **Success criteria**

## 6. STAR-SME Answer Model

Go beyond conventional STAR:

- **S — Situation:** What was happening?
- **T — Task:** What did I personally own?
- **A — Action:** What exactly did I do?
- **R — Result:** What changed?
- **S — SME Insight:** What trade-off did I make, what did I learn, and what would I recommend next time?

## 7. Interviewer Probe Ladder

1. Recall — What is it?
2. Understanding — How does it work?
3. Application — How have you used it?
4. Scenario — What would you do if…?
5. Trade-off — Why that approach?
6. Failure — What happens if it fails?
7. Scale — How would you handle it globally?
8. Strategy — What would you recommend for the future?

## 8. Mastery Progression

| Level | Candidate demonstrates |
|---|---|
| Foundation | Knows the terminology and concepts |
| Practitioner | Can perform the work |
| Problem Solver | Can diagnose and resolve |
| Senior Practitioner | Can handle complexity |
| SME | Can advise and influence |
| Strategic Advisor | Can shape future direction |

Depth should always be calibrated to the role, experience level and job description.

## 9. Job Description Mapping Method

Before creating a domain-specific Baisi Pahacha pack:

1. Parse the JD: must-have skills, responsibilities, experience, technology, domain, leadership, AI and outcomes.
2. Map requirements to the 22 Pahacha.
3. Identify the critical Pahacha for the role.
4. Create scenario questions around the highest-value requirements.
5. Map the candidate's actual experience to those scenarios.
6. Run mock interviews and identify gaps.

## 10. The 22 × 10 Architecture

**22 Pahacha × 10 scenarios = 220 scenarios**.

A full preparation pack can therefore contain 220 scenario questions, 220 STAR-SME answer models, follow-up probes, rapid-fire questions, 22 mastery frameworks, 22 anti-pattern sets, 22 success criteria sets and mock interview simulations.

The objective is **not memorization**. The objective is transferable professional reasoning.

## 11. Experience Evidence Bank

For each major project, capture:

- Business problem
- Personal role and responsibility
- Technology
- Design decision
- Configuration / development
- Integration
- Data
- Testing
- Incident or challenge
- Stakeholder conflict
- Result
- Lesson learned

One strong project story can support multiple Pahacha.

## 12. Quality Principles

- **Experience First** — Use real project experience wherever possible.
- **Scenario First** — Prefer realistic situations over definition-only questions.
- **Business Connected** — Connect technology to outcomes.
- **Depth Over Buzzwords** — Reward understanding, not jargon.
- **Trade-offs Matter** — Explain alternatives and consequences.
- **Personal Ownership** — Distinguish personal contribution from team activity.
- **Evidence Matters** — Use measurable results wherever available.
- **AI With Accountability** — Retain human judgment, privacy and governance.
- **Architecture Beyond Silos** — Consider process, data, application, technology, security and integration.
- **Continuous Improvement** — Every interview should reveal the next learning opportunity.

## 13. What BAISI PAHACHA Is Not

BAISI PAHACHA is not a memorization technique, a fixed list of answers, a certification replacement, a guarantee of interview selection, or a substitute for hands-on experience.

It is a **structured professional mastery framework**.
