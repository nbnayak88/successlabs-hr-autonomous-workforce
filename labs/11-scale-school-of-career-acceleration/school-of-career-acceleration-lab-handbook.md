# School of Career Acceleration Lab for Excellence — General Handbook

**Lab:** School of Career Acceleration Lab for Excellence (SCALE)  
**Track:** 10 — Career Acceleration  
**Learning Intent:** **POSITION**  
**Maturity Stage:** Intermediate  
**Core Intention:** Help professionals position themselves clearly in the job market.  
**Student Outcome:** Build strong professional identity and confidence.

> **Talent that cannot be seen cannot fully serve.** SCALE develops the ability to articulate value, demonstrate evidence, communicate a professional identity and deliberately design the next stage of a career.

---

# 1. Purpose

SCALE converts accumulated professional capability into **visible, credible and market-relevant positioning**.

The career acceleration loop is:

**Discover Capability → Evidence → Position → Communicate → Interview → Evaluate → Improve → Advance**

SCALE is not about self-promotion for its own sake. It is about **honest professional visibility** so that the right opportunities can recognize the right capabilities.

---

# 2. SCALE Philosophy

1. **Position is your intent** — make your professional value clear.
2. **Build a story, not just a resume** — communicate a coherent narrative of impact.
3. **Brand with authenticity** — represent experience honestly.
4. **Prepare to be interviewed and to interview** — career conversations are two-way evaluations.
5. **Strategize your next move** — design career progression deliberately.
6. **Rise at the Intermediate level** — capability exists; now learn to showcase it.
7. **Target across the 11 GICS sectors** — positioning should reflect the context you want to serve.
8. **Your rise serves the mission** — greater professional reach can create greater capacity for contribution.

# 6. Capability-to-Evidence Model

Never position a capability without evidence.

Use:

**Capability → Context → Action → Outcome → Evidence**

Example:

> **Capability:** Enterprise Architecture  
> **Context:** Multi-system transformation  
> **Action:** Designed target-state architecture and governance model  
> **Outcome:** Created a common decision framework across stakeholders  
> **Evidence:** Architecture blueprint, decision records and stakeholder outcomes

This is stronger than:

> “Experienced Enterprise Architect.”

---

# 8. Resume Architecture

A market-facing resume should make evidence discoverable.

For important experiences, use:

**Action + Context + Complexity + Outcome + Evidence**

Avoid duty-only statements.

### Weak
> Responsible for SAP implementation activities.

### Evidence-oriented
> Led solution realization for a multi-country SAP transformation, coordinating business and technology stakeholders and establishing controlled implementation and validation practices.

Where measurable metrics are available, use them accurately.

# 12. STAR + Evidence Interview Model

Use:

**S — Situation**  
What was happening?

**T — Task**  
What responsibility did you own?

**A — Action**  
What did you actually do?

**R — Result**  
What changed?

Then add:

**L — Learning**  
What did you learn?

**E — Evidence**  
What artifact, metric or outcome supports the story?

This creates a reusable **Interview Story Bank**.

# 16. Career Troubleshooting

### Strong skills, weak visibility
Build evidence and communicate expertise consistently.

### Strong resume, weak interview performance
Increase scenario practice and structured storytelling.

### Many certifications, limited practical evidence
Convert learning into projects, case studies and demonstrable outcomes.

### Broad experience, unclear positioning
Identify the strongest recurring capability/problem combination.

### Strong technical depth, weak business narrative
Translate technology into business outcomes.

### Strong experience, outdated profile
Refresh positioning, evidence and professional narrative.

### Frequent applications, low response
Review role targeting, evidence alignment and positioning clarity rather than simply increasing application volume.

# 20. SCALE Rule Book — Operationalized

### Do
- Craft your career as a story of value.
- Build evidence behind capabilities.
- Brand yourself authentically.
- Prepare to answer and ask questions.
- Design your career path intentionally.
- Showcase skills confidently.
- Tailor positioning to target roles and sectors.
- Own achievements honestly.
- Seek feedback.
- Build a clear professional identity.

### Don't
- List duties without outcomes.
- Exaggerate or fabricate experience.
- Treat branding as performance without substance.
- Treat interviews as one-way interrogations.
- Drift passively without direction.
- Undersell genuine capability.
- Use one generic pitch for every context.
- Hide legitimate achievements.
- Ignore feedback.
- Confuse visibility with capability.
