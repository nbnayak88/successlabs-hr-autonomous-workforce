# AWF1 — Interview Simulator Blueprint

## Applied SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central

**Purpose:** Turn the completed AWF1 interview scenario bank into an adaptive interview-practice, assessment and mastery experience.

**Transformation spine:**

**Employee → HR Foundation → Person & Employment Data → Organization → Job & Position → HR Process → Experience → Integration → Insight → Automation → Intelligent HR Outcome**

**Product posture:** SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central-first.

**Boundary:** AWF1 owns Employee Central and the digital HR foundation. Recruiting, Onboarding, Succession, Performance, Compensation, Time, Payroll and other HR academies remain separate modules.

---

## 1. Simulator Learning Loop

**SELECT → THINK → SPEAK → PROBE → SCORE → REFLECT → REATTEMPT**

The learner answers before seeing the reference STAR answer.

### Candidate response contract

1. Situation
2. Task
3. Action
4. Result
5. Architecture Signal
6. Product Signal
7. Business Signal
8. SME Depth

## 2. Canonical Content Pool

**22 Themes × 20 Scenarios = 440 scenarios**

Each scenario follows:

**Interview Question → STAR Answer → SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central Example → SME Probe**

Stable IDs:

**HR-AWF1-B[THEME]-Q[01–20]**

## 3. Assessment Rubric — 100 Points

| Dimension | Points |
|---|---:|
| Situation | 10 |
| Task | 10 |
| Action | 20 |
| Result | 15 |
| Architecture | 15 |
| Product | 10 |
| Business Value | 10 |
| SME Probe | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

### Readiness

- 90–100: Architect-level
- 80–89: Strong
- 70–79: Good — targeted improvement
- 60–69: Borderline
- <60: Rebuild required

Critical security, privacy, data ownership, product-boundary or architecture failures can override the numeric score.

## 8. Adaptive Scenario Selection

| Factor | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Weakness | 35% |
| Theme / progression need | 20% |
| Difficulty fit | 15% |
| Recency / repetition | 10% |
| Diversity | 10% |
| Controlled randomness | 10% |

Rules:

- No immediate scenario repeat.
- Prioritize demonstrated weaknesses.
- Do not overtrain mastered capabilities.
- Preserve breadth across all 22 themes.
- Increase difficulty only when evidence supports it.
- Re-test weak capabilities later.

---

## 9. Mastery State

**NEW → PRACTICED → DEVELOPING → MASTERED**

Mastery requires repeated evidence of:

**STAR + Product + Architecture + Business Outcome + SME Depth**

A later weak performance can return a mastered capability to developing.

## 12. Anti-Memorization

Vary:

- Scenario order
- SME probe
- Difficulty
- HR business context
- Global/local constraints
- Architecture constraints
- Data and integration dependencies
- Transformation objectives

The learner must demonstrate **HR architecture reasoning**, not reproduce scripts.

---

## 13. Feedback Contract

Every attempt returns:

### What You Did Well
Strong elements of the response.

### What Was Missing
Missing STAR, product, architecture or business reasoning.

### Architect Upgrade
How to elevate the response.

### Retry Challenge
A targeted variation or follow-up.

---

## 14. Reflection

After meaningful attempts:

1. What did I know?
2. What did I miss?
3. What assumption did I make?
4. What architecture decision did I make?
5. What HR/business outcome did I optimize?
6. What would I change?
