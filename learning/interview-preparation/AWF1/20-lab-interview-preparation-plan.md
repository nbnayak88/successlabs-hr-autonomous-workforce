## Interview Spine

Lab 11 is the formal interview-preparation assessment layer.

It uses the 22 BAISI PAHACHA themes: KNOW 01–04, DESIGN 05–08, DELIVER 09–12, SOLVE 13–16, INFLUENCE 17–19, TRANSFORM 20–22.

Target: 22 themes × 20 unique STAR scenarios = 440 AWF1 interview scenario positions.

## No-Duplicate Gate

Before creating a scenario:
1. Search the AWF1 repository.
2. Search the Autonomous HR master repository.
3. Search existing SCALE / interview folders.
4. Search related HR stream repositories for equivalent cross-stream scenarios.
5. Map reusable content.
6. Create only the uncovered scenario.

Stable ID: HR-AWF1-B[Theme]-Q[01-20]

## Completion Rule

A lab can be marked Complete only when its planned evidence exists and is linked from the tracker.

Lab 11 cannot be marked Complete merely because 440 questions exist. It requires BAISI theme coverage, unique scenario IDs, STAR answers, SME probes, reflection, architecture signal, duplicate check, source reference and measurable competency outcome.
