# AWF1 — Theme 01: Domain Foundation

## Purpose
Assess whether the candidate understands HCM as a business and operating domain before selecting a technology or product.

## Quality standard
Questions must be architecture- and problem-led, not SAP-product trivia. SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central may be used as an implementation example, but the underlying question must remain relevant to HCM practitioners and architects across platforms.

## Theme scope
- HCM operating model and purpose
- Employee lifecycle / hire-to-retire
- workforce structures
- HR operating processes
- roles of HR, managers, employees and HR technology
- global vs local HR requirements
- core HR as a foundation for downstream processes
- business outcomes and value

## Question construction standard
Each approved question will contain:
1. Realistic business situation
2. Candidate task
3. Strong-answer reasoning
4. STAR answer
5. Architecture signals
6. SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central example where useful
7. SME probe
8. Reflection
9. Measurable business outcome

## Quality gates
- Not product-trivia driven
- Answerable without knowing SAP SuccessFactors
- Product examples strengthen the answer rather than define it
- Tests judgment, not memorization
- Contains meaningful trade-offs
- Suitable for consultant / architect interviews
- No duplicate with another AWF1 question
- Clear business outcome
